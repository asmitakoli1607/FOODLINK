NEW FEATURE: FARM-TO-FOOD-SOURCE MARKETPLACE
Purpose: farmers can sell fresh vegetables directly to hotels, canteens, caterers, and other food providers without a middleman.

1. Farmer logs in and opens Farmer Market.
2. Farmer adds a produce listing: vegetable name, available quantity, price per kg, farm location, and contact note.
3. Listing is stored in SQLite3 with status AVAILABLE and appears on the marketplace.
4. A Food Provider logs in, opens Farmer Market, and presses CONNECT & REQUEST.
5. Listing status becomes REQUESTED. The provider is saved as the buyer, and both parties can see the request on the market page.
6. Farmer and Provider can use the displayed contact note to complete the direct sale outside this prototype.
7. Admin can see every listing. Future version: price negotiation, online payments, delivery tracking, ratings, verified farms, and maps.

This feature supports FoodLink's circular ecosystem: farmers sell fresh produce to food sources; food sources can later redirect safe surplus meals to NGOs; unsuitable food can go to compost.
